This project, called Robotic Braille or Reading with Your Hands, focuses on the use of tactility for cognition and perception of objects and environments. The research aims to establish an understanding of tactility through transfers of images and information, towards surface patterns and textures, and integration of Braille text.
It uses GH Grasshopper code to explore design variability for points, grids and line configurations and a six-axis ABB robot equipped with different routing tools for milling in timber.
The project also involves the creation of multifunctional furniture objects that integrate both visual and tactile information for diverse audiences, using Universal Design for equitable and inclusive use. Through this work, the project seeks to increase awareness, knowledge, and understanding of other people’s conditions, contributing to more inclusive environments.
