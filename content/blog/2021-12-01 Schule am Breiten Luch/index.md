The School am Breiten Luch is the result of an architectural competition to design an integrated school for 725 pupils in the Lichtenberg district of Berlin. The school is designed according to a so-called compartment principle with small school units within the school. Two sports halls are to be integrated on a relatively small site, where there is heavy noise pollution from both a major road and a railway line. In addition, there is a water pipeline across the site that needs to be kept free of construction, which has required a different way of organising the construction.
