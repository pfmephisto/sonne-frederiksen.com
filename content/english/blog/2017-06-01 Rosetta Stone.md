The Rosetta Stone was a multi stage exercise in mapping. Starting out with a drawing based mapping of Anholt island, in three different scales, focusing on tourism as a resources, that in a following step where combined and abstracted in to a milling. The processes for creating the drawing included pulling locations from social media and mapping them on to a topographical map, visualizing the island how most visitors would experience it. In the following iteration that mapping was then transformed in to custom tooling paths for the final milling.
